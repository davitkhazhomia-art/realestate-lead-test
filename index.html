What a good system should do

1. Respond fast. Start the conversation while the lead is still paying attention.
2. Qualify. Collect budget, location, timeline and intent.
3. Follow up. Keep the conversation alive without manually remembering every lead.
4. Escalate. Put serious prospects in front of the agent quickly.
5. Book. Make the next step — call or showing — easy.

The simple flow

New lead → instant response → qualification → follow-up → appointment → human agent

The point isn't to replace the agent. It's to make sure a good lead doesn't disappear because nobody followed up.
